About Me

📋 Quick Info

 

I'm an AI Engineer and Computer Science graduate from EELU, Alexandria, passionate about bridging the gap between cutting-edge research and real-world applications. My work spans LLMs, computer vision, and end-to-end deployment pipelines.

I'm currently building HealthBridge, a multimodal medical AI platform powered by AWS Bedrock, FastAPI, and the ACE (Agentic Context Engineering) framework — featuring intelligent intake bots, diagnostic agents, and RAG-augmented clinical reasoning.

Beyond medical AI, I've worked on traffic sign detection, deepfake detection, face recognition systems, and time-series forecasting. I thrive at the intersection of research and production — taking models from notebooks to deployed systems.
